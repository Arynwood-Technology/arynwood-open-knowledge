# Research methodology
Separate engineering experiments, evidence syntheses and formal academic research.

Engineering reports evaluate software behavior against specified datasets. They do not establish instructional efficacy. Literature reviews document searches, inclusion/exclusion rules, dates, provider coverage, deduplication, screening decisions and appraisal. Formal research involving participants requires appropriate institutional review, consent and data governance before recruitment.

For each educational question, document population, context, intervention, comparison and outcome where applicable. Search multiple relevant sources; record exact strings and dates. Preserve negative and contradictory findings. Assess methodology, sampling, measurement validity, attrition, confounding, context, funding and limitations. Do not equate citation counts, identifiers, journal labels or statistical significance with reliable generalization.

Bibliographic verification checks selected fields against a provider record. Evidence appraisal requires reading lawful source material and human judgment. At least one named qualified reviewer must document how an interpretation follows from its sources; high-stakes recommendations need domain professional review. This app does not implement diagnosis, placement or IEP decisions.

Reproducible experiments must retain source license decisions, versioned protocols, environment locks, prompt/model hashes, deidentified annotations, metrics, error analysis and revisions. Dual independent annotations and adjudication are preferred for claim support. Report missing evidence and coverage biases. Do not redistribute full text or provider datasets without rights review.
