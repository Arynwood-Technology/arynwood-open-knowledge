# Editorial and corrections policy
All content begins as draft/unreviewed. Publication requires a named editor, completed source checks, attribution/license review, accessibility checks and a dated revision. Material instructional claims require qualified human domain review. Bibliographic verification, ORCID authentication and community popularity do not substitute for appraisal.

Identify articles as engineering technical report, literature review, educational resource, professional guidance or AI-assisted interpretation. Only use peer-reviewed when a specific documented review process supports that statement. A technical report is not automatically peer-reviewed. State conflicts of interest, authorship, model assistance and limitations.

Keep permanent article URLs and transparent history. Substantive corrections append a dated notice explaining the changed claim and supporting evidence; retain prior revisions where lawful. Withdraw unsafe or unsupported guidance with a public notice; redact private data immediately without republishing it in history. Provide a correction issue template and reviewer tracking.

Publishing is planned, not implemented. Future flow: contribution → editorial queue → rights/evidence/accessibility checks → human approval → explicit public export → static article build. No default publish-on-save and no automatic publication of AI output. Authors control public affiliation disclosure. Student records are never used for SEO, public citations or research APIs.
